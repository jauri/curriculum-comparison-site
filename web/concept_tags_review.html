Concept tags

Proposed tags in data/reference/<school>/concept_tags.csv, one school at a time, for review.

What this shows. Each in-scope course (CS, named, the school's own catalog text) with its description and the concepts it is tagged with. Every tag's evidence is a phrase copied from the title or description; it is highlighted in the text, and hovering a tag row outlines its phrase. A tag means the concept is mentioned in the course's description, not that it is or isn't taught. Confidence is high when the phrase names the concept directly, medium when it takes a short step (e.g. "structured programming" for control structures), and low when the mention is passing or uncertain. To change a tag, edit the CSV (set tag_status to approved or rejected), run src/check_concepts.py, then src/preview_concepts.py.
