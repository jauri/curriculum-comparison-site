Credits by area

How many credits each school's CS degree requires in each subject area.

How to read this. Each chart is one subject area, and each column is one school's required credits in that area, from its published plan. UC is the first column; the other schools follow from most credits to fewest. The dashed line is the median of the other schools. A lighter cap on a column means the plan gives a range (for example, a 4–5 credit science course). Each chart has its own scale. Hover a column for details. Click or tap a column (or any number in the table at the bottom) to see the evidence: a link to the school's published plan, the page that states its degree total, and every course and slot that adds up to the count.
What's counted. Every row of the plan that counts toward the degree total, grouped by subject area (the same areas and colors as Course Plans). Co-op work terms carry no credit. Requirement slots (a "CS elective", a gen-ed choice) count in the area of the slot.
