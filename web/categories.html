Course categories

When each school schedules the courses in each major category of computing, and where UC differs.

How to read this. Each shaded band is one category of computing courses, and each row inside it is one canonical course, drawn as on the Timeline page. A dot is one school's required course, placed in its semester of a 4-year plan (UC's 5-year co-op plan is normalized to 8 semesters); dots in the same semester stack. The bar under each name is the share of the other schools that require the course (lighter: only as one option of a choice). Schools that don't require it sit in the Course not required bin. UC is the blue dot with a ring. The last column flags where UC differs from most other schools: ◀ earlier, ▶ later, ✕ missing, or ◆ few others require it. Hover a dot to follow that school down the page; click to keep following it.
What's shown. Computing courses in six categories, within a category ordered by the semester most schools take them. Courses no school requires are left out. Computing courses not in any category: none. Generic descriptions are marked generic.
