Concept alignment

UC's required courses next to another school's, joined by the concepts their catalog descriptions share.

How to read this. Courses are placed as in the Alignment tab: by semester of a 4-year plan, with box height proportional to credits. Each CS course's concepts (from our tags of its catalog description) are split evenly among its concepts. A concept that also appears in a course at the other school sends its share there as a band; if it appears in two of the other school's courses, its share is split between them. The column on each box's inner edge shows where the course's concepts went: one segment per band, and a hatched segment for concepts found in none of the other school's courses. The two ends of a band can differ in width, because each end is a share of its own course (two shared concepts may be half of one course and a fifth of another).
Mentioned, not taught. A concept counts only if a required course's catalog description mentions it. Descriptions are short, so a concept that isn't mentioned may still be taught. Only CS courses with the school's own description are tagged; other rows are drawn faded as "not assessed".

Click a course to pin it and see where each of its concepts went.
