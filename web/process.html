The full working conversation between the project's author and Claude, an AI assistant, from the first idea to the current pages.

How to read this. Time runs down the page. The author's messages are on the left; Claude's replies and the work it did between messages are on the right. A gray actions card sums up everything Claude did before its next reply (fetching catalog pages, writing scripts, running checks); open it for the full list. Where Claude asked a multiple-choice question, the author's choice appears on the left. Black bands mark where each step of the project plan began, and small lines mark each saved version (git commit). Text is exactly as written; long messages open with Show more. Claude's private reasoning and the raw output of its tools are not shown. Times are U.S. Eastern.
