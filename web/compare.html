Curriculum alignment

UC's required courses next to another school's, in normalized semester order.

How to read this. Each box is a required course or requirement slot, placed in its semester of a 4-year plan (UC's 5-year co-op plan is normalized to 8 semesters; co-op work terms are left out unless Show co-op terms is on). Box height is proportional to credits. A line joins two courses that our reviewed matching assigns to the same canonical course (a course may match more than one); dashed lines are partial matches, and dotted lines are possible matches through one option of a choice (the student may take another). A line that slopes down means the other school takes that course later than UC. Hover to highlight a course and its counterparts; click to pin it and see details. Faded boxes have no counterpart at the other school.
Generic descriptions are marked generic. They are typical descriptions of a course or requirement with that name, written for this project, not the school's own catalog text.
Co-op terms. With Show co-op terms on, each school's co-op work terms appear in their place between academic semesters (shaded rows), and a school with no co-op term at that point gets an empty "No co-op term" box, so semester 3 is still across from semester 3. Each side is then labeled with its own school's terms: UC's semester 3 is its Year 2 Spring, while a school without co-op takes semester 3 in Year 2 Fall.

Click a course to pin it and see its description, canonical course, and counterparts.
