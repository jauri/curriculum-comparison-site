Curriculum editor

Draft revised versions of UC's curriculum, then compare each draft with UC as it is and with another school.

How to edit. Drag a course to another semester to move it, or into Removed to take it out of the plan (on a touch screen, press and hold a card, then drag). Click a course to change its title, description, credits, or semester, and use Add a course for a new one. Every change is saved in this browser right away; use Export to keep a copy or move your drafts to another device. The draft covers UC's 8 academic semesters; co-op work terms are not editable. Switch to All terms, with co-op to see UC's real 5-year calendar, with each semester under its own year and term and the co-op terms in between (all three sections). Only CS courses can be moved, removed, or revised, and added courses are CS courses; math, science, engineering, writing, and general education stay as UC has them (click one to view it).
Checks. The editor warns (but doesn't stop you) when a semester's credits fall outside the limits you set, when a course comes before or in the same semester as a prerequisite (or its prerequisite is removed), or when a course is placed before the class standing it requires. Prerequisites are read from UC's catalog; only those within UC's own plan can be checked, and added courses have none.
Descriptions. Text you write in the editor is marked proposed: it is not UC's catalog text. Generic descriptions, written for this project, stay marked generic.
