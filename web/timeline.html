Course timeline

When each school schedules each canonical course in a 4-year plan, and which schools don't require it.

How to read this. Each strip is one canonical course, the shared list of course concepts our reviewed matching assigns every school's courses to. A dot is one school's required course or requirement slot, placed in its semester of a 4-year plan (UC's 5-year co-op plan is normalized to 8 semesters). Dots in the same semester stack. The small bar under each name shows the share of the other 15 schools that require the course (lighter: only as one option of a choice). Schools with no required course matched to the strip sit in the Course not required bin on the right. UC is the blue dot with a ring; every other school is gray. Hover a dot for the school, course, and description; the school's dots in every other strip light up too. Click a dot (or pick a school) to keep following that school.
What's shown. Computing (CS and IT), Engineering & co-op prep, and Math & statistics courses only; lab science, writing, and general education are left out. Co-op work terms have no academic semester and are not drawn. Generic descriptions are marked generic: typical descriptions of a course with that name, written for this project, not the school's own catalog text.
