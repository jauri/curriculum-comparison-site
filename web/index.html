How the 5-year co-op plan becomes 4 years. UC alternates academic semesters with paid co-op work semesters. The 5 co-op terms are removed and the 8 academic terms are numbered 1–8 in order, including one academic summer. Fall and Spring below are by position (odd = Fall), so they don't always match UC's calendar season. For example, UC's Spring 2028 becomes Year 2 Fall.
