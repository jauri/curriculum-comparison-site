Concept tags

Proposed tags in data/reference/<school>/concept_tags.csv, one school at a time, for review.

What this shows. Each in-scope course (CS, named, the school's own catalog text) with its description and the concepts it is tagged with. Every tag's evidence is a phrase copied from the title or description; it is highlighted in the text, and hovering a tag row outlines its phrase. A tag means the concept is mentioned in the course's description, not that it is or isn't taught. Every phrase that states a concept is listed and highlighted. Text that is underlined with dots was looked at and judged not to be a concept (a course label, framing, a prerequisite...); hover it for the reason, or open "Text not tagged" under the course. A solid underline marks a topic the vocabulary has no concept for. Confidence is high when the phrase names the concept directly, medium when it takes a short step (e.g. "structured programming" for control structures), and low when the mention is passing or uncertain. To change a tag, edit the CSV (set tag_status to approved or rejected), run src/check_concepts.py, then src/preview_concepts.py. Reasons for untagged text are in untagged_notes.csv, checked by src/check_untagged.py.
